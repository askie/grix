Support paths

  • Use in-app help and feedback options when app access is available.
  • Email support: gcf@dhfeng.com
  • If you installed the app from an app store, you may also use the support channel listed there.
  • If this app was distributed by a private deployment, contact the operator of that deployment.

Common requests

  • Account deletion or inability to access the in-app deletion flow.
  • Privacy or data use questions.
  • Login, registration, push notification, or content sync issues.

What to include

  • Your username or account identifier.
  • The platform you are using, such as iOS or Android.
  • A concise description of the problem and the steps already tried.
  • A reply email address if you need direct follow-up.
This support page intentionally avoids dependency on third-party hosting and is served directly from the backend domain used by the app.
If your request is specifically about account deletion, start with the account deletion page.
