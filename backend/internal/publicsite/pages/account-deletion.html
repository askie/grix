Account & Data Deletion

This page is the public web resource for requesting deletion of a Grix account and associated user data. You can submit the request here without reinstalling or opening the mobile app.

Effective date: November 6, 2026

Request account deletion by email Privacy policy Support

Request deletion on the web

Email support@grix.im with the subject Grix account deletion request. Include enough information for us to identify the account, such as username, registered email or phone number, and the platform you used.

  1. Send the deletion request to support@grix.im.
  2. We verify account ownership before completing deletion.
  3. We process verified requests and confirm when the account and associated data have been deleted or anonymized.

This web pathway does not require you to download, open, or sign in to the Grix mobile app.

Optional in-app deletion

If you still have access to the app, you may also delete the account directly inside Grix:

  1. Sign in with the account you want to delete.
  2. Open account settings or the privacy section.
  3. Choose delete account and confirm the request.

What is deleted

  • Login access is revoked and the account is disabled.
  • User-owned personal records are scrubbed or removed.
  • Device push bindings, refresh tokens, and account-side relationships are revoked.
  • Agent secrets owned by the account are cleared.

What may be retained

  • Some historical or system-level references may be anonymized for integrity, abuse prevention, or legal compliance.
  • Content you shared with other users may remain visible to those recipients in anonymized form.
  • Deletion is irreversible once completed.
