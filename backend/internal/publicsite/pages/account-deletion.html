Grix

Account Deletion

Grix supports full account deletion directly inside the app. This action is intended to satisfy app store account deletion requirements and is irreversible.

Effective date: March 8, 2026

Privacy policy Support

How to delete your account

  1. Sign in to the app with the account you want to delete.
  2. Open the account settings or privacy section.
  3. Choose the delete account action and confirm the request.

What happens after deletion

  • The account is disabled and user-owned personal records are scrubbed or removed.
  • Push bindings, refresh tokens, and account-side relationships are revoked.
  • Some historical or system-level references may be anonymized for integrity reasons.

Important notes

  • Deletion is irreversible.
  • You should export or retain any information you need before confirming deletion.
  • If you cannot access the app, review the support page for fallback contact guidance.

If you cannot log in

If normal in-app deletion is unavailable because you cannot access the account, use the support page for escalation guidance and include enough information for the operator of this deployment to identify your account.
